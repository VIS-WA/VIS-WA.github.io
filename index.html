About

// research statement

Hello! I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Ambuj Varshney.

I explore how next-generation embedded tags are evolving toward architectures that emphasize local processing and peer-to-peer coordination — enabling energy-sustainable operation at scale.

A second thread of my work asks how smaller foundational models can bring practical on-device AI to highly constrained IoT environments, where memory, power, and connectivity are all in short supply. Together these directions sit under one umbrella: making wireless embedded systems more capable, more autonomous, and far more efficient.
